# Forms: Close a form

{% hint style="warning" %}
This article applies to **Form (Legacy)**. For the current form editor, see [Forms](README.md).
{% endhint %}

Set criteria for when a form should stop accepting answers.

Use this when a form should only run for a limited time or up to a maximum number of submissions.

## Open the form's publish settings

1.  In the campaign view, click the **Publish** icon on the form's card.

    <div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/close-a-form-form-card-publish-icon.png" alt="Form card in the campaign view with an arrow pointing to the Publish icon"></div>
2.  Open the menu next to the form name at the top of the page and click **Open/Close Form**.

    <div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/close-a-form-form-menu-open-close.png" alt="Menu next to the form name with an arrow pointing to Open/Close Form"></div>
3.  Choose the settings you need for your form, then click **Save Changes**.

    <div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/close-a-form-open-close-settings.png" alt="Open/Close settings with visitor settings, open and close options, and the closed-form message editor"></div>
