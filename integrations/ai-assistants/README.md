# AI assistants

Connect Claude to your eMarketeer account and ask about your marketing in plain language.

The assistant works with your real data: reports, contacts, journeys, campaigns and forms. It reads from the accounts you belong to. Every user on an account can read this data through the connector, whatever their role in eMarketeer.

## What you can do

Ask about your results, look up contacts and check how your journeys are running. Marketing Users and Administrators can also ask the assistant to make changes, such as creating a campaign or closing a form.

## Examples

Write to the assistant the way you would ask a colleague.

### Reports

- How did our emails perform last month compared with the month before?
- Which pages on our website got the most visits this week?
- Export last quarter's email results as a spreadsheet.

### Contacts

- Give me a short brief on anna@example.com before my sales call.
- Why is anna@example.com's lead score so high?
- Has anna@example.com agreed to receive emails?

### Journeys

- Are any of our journeys failing? What's going wrong?
- Who is waiting in the second step of the onboarding journey?

### Campaigns and forms

- Create a campaign called Spring webinar in the Events folder.
- Show how many people chose each answer in our customer survey form.
- Close the registration form for the June event.

### Planning

- Suggest our next campaign based on what has worked recently.
- Check our email deliverability and tell me what to fix.

## Who can do what

| What | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Read reports, contacts, journeys, campaigns and forms | Every user |
| Change things: tags, notes, segments, campaigns, starting or restarting journeys, opening or closing forms | Marketing Users and Administrators on that account |

If you don't have one of these roles on any of your accounts, the assistant doesn't offer these actions at all.

## Supported assistants

Claude works on claude.ai and in the Claude desktop app. See [Connect Claude to eMarketeer](connect-claude.md).
