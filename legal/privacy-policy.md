# Privacy Policy

## eMarketeer Privacy Policy

### Legal Compliance

eMarketeer complies with REGULATION (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and Council (GDPR), which governs the processing and protection of personal data and its free movement across the EU.

### General Principles and Governance

**Data Processing Agreements**

We enter into Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with our customers to define how we process personal data on their behalf. We also establish sub-processing agreements with any third-party processors we use.

**What Information Do We Collect?**

We collect company details, contact names, email addresses, locations, and phone numbers during customer registration.

Third-party vendors like Google may use cookies to deliver ads based on user visits. Users can opt out via the [Google Ads Settings](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

**What Do We Use Your Information For?**

Information we collect may be used to:

* Process transactions
* Identify and support customers
* Contact you in case of account issues
* Administer promotions, surveys, or site features
* Send updates on product releases and news

Your contact details will only be used for relevant communication. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information without consent, except to deliver services you’ve requested.

**Risk Management**

We perform annual risk assessments related to personal data, guided by GDPR Article 24 and ICT regulations §3.

**Information Classification and Control**

We classify and safeguard data based on criticality. Each data asset has a designated owner responsible for maintenance and access control. All data is treated as confidential, securely stored, and only accessible by the customer and authorized personnel.

**Data Retention**

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for its intended purpose.

**Rights and Contact Information**

You can request to access, correct, delete, or export your data at any time. Contact our Data Protection Officer at [privacy@emarketeer.com](mailto:privacy@emarketeer.com).

**How Do We Protect Your Information?**

We use secure servers and SSL encryption to protect your sensitive data during transmission. Only authorized users can access stored information. We do not store credit card or financial details after processing.

**Do We Use Cookies?**

Yes. Cookies help us understand site usage and improve user experience. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

**Do We Disclose Any Information to Outside Parties?**

No. We do not share personal information except with trusted partners who assist in delivering our services under confidentiality agreements, or when required by law. Non-personal data may be used for analytics and marketing.

**California Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance**

We do not distribute personal data without consent, in accordance with CalOPPA.

**Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance**

We do not collect information from individuals under 13 years of age. Our website is intended for users aged 13 and older.

**Terms of Service**

For more details on using our website, please review our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

**Your Consent**

By filling out a form (e.g., for newsletters, demos, or downloads), you consent to our processing of your information. You can withdraw consent at any time.
