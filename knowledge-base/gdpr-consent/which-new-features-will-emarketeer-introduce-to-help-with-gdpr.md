# Which new features will eMarketeer introduce to help with GDPR?

eMarketeer introduces Consent Management and Subscription Categories to support GDPR compliance.

Consent Management is the primary GDPR feature. Subscription Categories let your audience fine-tune the communication they receive from you.

## Consent Management

Consent needs to be tied to a purpose. From the start, consent contains three purposes:

* Store data — to store a contact's information in the database.
* E-marketing — to send marketing material.
* Profiling — to store information such as behaviour, interests, and location.

The features in eMarketeer to manage consent include:

* Set consent (purpose, legal base, and source) when creating new contacts through SuperOffice import, file import, and manual creation.
* Bulk-update consent on selections of contacts.
* A new consent block in forms for visitors to give consent.
* Complete and enhanced logging of changes made to contacts and consent.

## Overview and mockups of new features

### Account settings

In the account settings, you manage how you deal with consent. For example, you can choose to block all marketing send-outs if consent is missing or has been withdrawn. This new setting replaces the unsubscription center. You can also add subscription categories here.

### Contact card – subscription and consent

In the contact card, you find a switch for marketing send-outs and a list of the subscription categories the contact belongs to. A new consent tab shows the consent for each purpose and a consent history log for the contact.

### File import

With a single click during a file import, you set the consent and purpose that should apply to all contacts in the file.

### Filters

The new filter settings let you sort contacts by consent, purpose, subscription category, dates, and more.

### Bulk updating

Update several contacts at once to set consent or purpose. You can also bulk-update subscriptions.

### Consent in forms

A new consent block is added to the form builder. Add the consent text, choose the purpose for this form, and contacts can give consent themselves. The legal base is automatically set to consent.

### Subscription categories

Consent to send e-marketing is valid for all types of marketing material. For that material to stay relevant, contacts should have the final say in what they receive. That is why eMarketeer introduces Subscription Categories.

This is not part of the GDPR requirements as such, but the feature is likely to reduce unsubscription rates and keep consent in place longer, because contacts only receive information they are interested in.

When you build an email, you pick which subscription category the send-out is relevant to. The email is then sent only to contacts in that category.

### Sending emails

When you hit send, you see an additional option to override consent. Tick this box only with great caution. The send-out checklist also shows the number of contacts who have unsubscribed and the number where consent is missing or has been withdrawn.

### Subscription center

This center replaces the standard unsubscribe page used in all eMarketeer templates. It lets the subscriber change which subscriptions to receive or unsubscribe from all marketing send-outs.

If the subscriber checks the box to unsubscribe from all send-outs, this withdraws consent for e-marketing.

### New automations

Consent and subscriptions can be updated automatically based on a specific action or trigger you set in eMarketeer.
