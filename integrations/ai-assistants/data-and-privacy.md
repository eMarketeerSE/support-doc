# AI assistants: data and privacy

What an AI assistant can see in your eMarketeer account, and where the data goes.

## What the assistant can see

The assistant reads from the accounts you belong to. Every user on an account can read its data through the connector, whatever their role in eMarketeer. Changes need the Marketing User or Administrator role on that account.

## Where your data goes

When you ask a question, the data needed to answer it is sent to the AI provider you connected. That can include contact details and report figures. Your agreement with that provider covers how they handle it.

eMarketeer doesn't keep anything extra. Spreadsheet exports you ask for are deleted after one day.

## Personal data

You remain responsible for the personal data in your eMarketeer account. Before you share contact data with an AI assistant, check that your agreement with the provider covers processing personal data.

## Disconnect

You can disconnect at any time. See [Connect Claude to eMarketeer](connect-claude.md#disconnect) or [Connect ChatGPT to eMarketeer](connect-chatgpt.md#disconnect).
