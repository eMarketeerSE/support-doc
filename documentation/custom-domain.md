# Custom domain

The custom domain is part of the unique links to content you create in eMarketeer.

By default, your domain name is **app.emarketeer.com**. This appears in all URLs generated by eMarketeer, such as forms, landing pages, and email tracking links.

### Using a via-em.com domain as a custom domain

This is the preferred method, because it supports the HTTPS protocol. You can freely use any unused subdomain of our via-em.com domain to brand your account. The subdomain must be unused by another eMarketeer account.

Example: https://**yourcompany**.via-em.com/
