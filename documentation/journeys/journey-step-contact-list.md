# Contact List

The Contact List step adds the contact to a contact list, or removes them from one.

<div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/journey-step-contact-list-settings.png" alt="Contact List dialog with the Add to contact list and Remove from contact list options and a field to choose the list"></div>

## Settings

* Choose **Add to contact list** or **Remove from contact list**.
* Choose the contact list.

Click **Apply** to save the step.

To learn how to create a contact list, see [How to Create a New Contact List](../../knowledge-base/getting-started/new-contact-list.md).
