# Remove from Campaign automation

The Remove from Campaign automation is a [campaign automation](campaign-automations.md) that removes the contact who triggered it from a campaign. The contact itself is not deleted.

<div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/campaign-automation-remove-from-campaign-settings.png" alt="Remove from Campaign automation settings with the name, the campaign to remove the contact from and Do only once per contact"></div>

## Settings

* **Name** — a name for the automation, shown in the campaign's **Automation rules** list.
* **Select the campaign to remove contact from** — choose the campaign.
* **Do only once per contact** — selected by default, so the automation runs at most once for each contact.

## Trigger

Choose the component and event that run the automation under **When this happens**. See [Choose the trigger](campaign-automations.md#choose-the-trigger).
