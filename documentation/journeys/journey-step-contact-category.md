# Contact Category

The Contact Category step changes the contact's category.

<div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/journey-step-contact-category-options.png" alt="Contact Category dialog with the options Prospect, Customer and Other"></div>

## Settings

* **Update contact category** — choose **Prospect**, **Customer** or **Other**.

Click **Apply** to save the step.
