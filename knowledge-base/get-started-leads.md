# Get started with leads

**eMarketeer leads allows marketing to define qualified leads based on the engagement and personas and deliver them to the relevant sales team continuously in real time. The lead board gives sales an intuitive way to qualify and work with leads towards the sale.**

This guide will cover

1.  Setting up the sales teams and deliver qualified leads to them.
2.  As a sales user, how to work with the leads.
3.  Using eMarketeer Leads in your CRM

## ![](../assets/get-started-leads/theboardclean-1024x576.png)

## Generate and deliver leads to a sales team

The qualification and delivery of leads require these parts to be set up.

-   **Sales teams**  
    Leads can only be delivered to sales teams. You can have one or as many teams as you like.  
    [Create a sales team](https://support.emarketeer.com/knowledgebase/sales-teams/)
-   **Lead streams**  
    A lead stream is  a set of qualification rules (read “filter”). Any contact that matches the filter will be qualified as a lead and be delivered to the selected sales team(s).  
    [Create lead streams](https://support.emarketeer.com/knowledgebase/lead-streams/)
-   **Sales users**  
    “Sales” is a type of user which has access to the lead board for the purpose of processing leads. A sales user is always member of a team in which the leads are delivered.  
    [Create sales users](https://support.emarketeer.com/knowledgebase/sales-users/)

## Working with the lead board

Once leads are generated, sales users can access the lead board to evaluate the enriched leads and take them down the funnel.
