## Next-Gen Tracking: Cookieless and Future-Proof

We have rebuilt our website tracking from the ground up with a lot of improvements. 

Most importantly the new tracking is completely cookieless, meaning that you will track a lot more traffic than before as more and more browsers are blocking cookie based tracking. 

In addition to being cookieless, the new tracking picks up as soon as a visitor enters your site for the first time. Every time the visitor comes back, the tracking continues and finally, when the visitor converts to a lead via your website form, all the tracking history will be stored on the new lead. This tells you where the lead came from initially and helps you understand which of your traffic generation initiatives performs best.

✅ Cookieless tracking to safeguard your data collection  
✅ Detailed traffic source tracking—know where your leads are coming from  
✅ Full contact web history logged upon form submission  
✅ Initiate tracking from email clicks and form submissions

#### What You Need to Know

A new tracking script will be required to unlock these features.

Existing tracking will continue temporarily but should be upgraded as soon as possible.
