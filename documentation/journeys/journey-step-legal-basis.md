# Legal Basis

The Legal Basis step sets the legal basis for the contact. You set it for two purposes: storing and processing the contact's data, and sending marketing to the contact.

<div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/journey-step-legal-basis-settings.png" alt="Legal Basis dialog with Consent chosen for both purposes, each with a consent text"></div>

## Settings

* **Purpose: Store and process** — the legal basis for storing and processing the contact's data.
* **Purpose: Marketing sendouts** — the legal basis for sending marketing to the contact.

For each purpose, choose the legal basis, for example **Consent**, and enter a text that describes it, such as the consent text from your form.

Click **Apply** to save the step.
