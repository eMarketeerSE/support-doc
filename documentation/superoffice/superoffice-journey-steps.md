# SuperOffice Journey Steps

SuperOffice Journey Steps let you create records and update data in SuperOffice as contacts move through a Journey in eMarketeer. Each step pushes information to your connected SuperOffice account — creating activities or sales, managing project and selection memberships, or updating interest codes on contacts.
