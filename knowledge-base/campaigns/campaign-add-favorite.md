# Creating a shortcut to a Campaign using Favourites

Favourites is a section in the left panel of the Campaigns page that holds shortcuts to campaigns you want quick access to.

Use it to pin campaigns you visit often or that are currently active, so they are one click away on the Campaigns page.

<div data-with-frame="true" align="left"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/campaign-add-favorite-favourites-in-campaigns-panel.png" alt="Favourites in the left panel of the Campaigns page"></div>

Location of Favourites on the Campaigns page

## Add a campaign to Favourites

1. Find the campaign in the Campaigns list, under All Campaigns or in its folder.
2. Click the star icon on the left side of the campaign's row.
