# Webinar: How to Build Mobile Apps in eMarketeer

This webinar will show you how to use eMarketeer to build and distribute your very own mobile web apps for your events.

Adding a mobile app to your event is a fantastic way of putting information right in the hands of your event participants. There is simply no better way of engaging your audience and keeping them up to date on what is going on at your event.

But building an app and getting it onto your audiences’ phones is a huge challenge. But not anymore. With eMarketeer you can easily build, publish and distribute mobile web apps that will rock your events.

We will cover:

– How to create and design mobile web apps in eMarketeer

– How to deliver apps to your audience

– How to utilize apps to make your event engaging
