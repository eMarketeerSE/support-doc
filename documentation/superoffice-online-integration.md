# SuperOffice Online integration

Integrating SuperOffice Online with eMarketeer is easy.

#### Requirements

-   A SuperOffice Online account (**Sales** and **Complete CRM** versions are supported)
-   A eMarketeer account

#### Actions performed during the integration setup.

For the integration to work properly, eMarketeer installs new items to SuperOffice such as web panels, fields, types etc. [Read more about these actions](https://support.emarketeer.com/documentation/actions-performed-during-set-up/)

#### Start the integration

To start the integration, log in to eMarketeer and click “Account” and “Plugins and Intergrations”. From there you will find the SuperOffice settings page.

![so-online-1](../assets/superoffice-online-integration/so-online-1.png)

1.  Check the online radio button and click “Apply changes”.
2.  A popup with the SuperOffice login screen appears. Fill out your login and click “Log in” to authenticate.
3.  Wait while the integration finishes and you’re done!
