# AI assistants: troubleshooting

Fixes for the most common problems when you use eMarketeer from an AI assistant.

## You can't log in with Microsoft or SuperOffice

The connector supports only email and password logins. Log in with your eMarketeer email address and password. If you don't have a password, ask your administrator.

## The assistant says it can't make changes

Changing things, such as creating a campaign or tagging a contact, needs the Marketing User or Administrator role on that account. If you have neither role on any of your accounts, the assistant doesn't offer these actions at all. Ask your administrator for the role.

## The wrong account answers, or the account isn't available

You belong to several accounts, so the assistant needs to know which one you mean. Name the account in your request, or ask: "Which eMarketeer accounts do I have access to?"

## A contact can't be found

The assistant finds contacts by their exact email address. Check the spelling of the address and try again.

## No website page figures for older dates

Visit figures per page start on 2 October 2026. They also need the web tracker on your website. See [Installing the web tracker script on your website](../../documentation/web-tracker/installing-the-web-tracker-script-on-your-website.md).

## The connector stopped working

Disconnect eMarketeer in your assistant's settings, then connect again. See [Connect Claude to eMarketeer](connect-claude.md) or [Connect ChatGPT to eMarketeer](connect-chatgpt.md).

## Still stuck

Contact eMarketeer support. Tell us which assistant you used, when it happened, which account you asked about and what you asked.
